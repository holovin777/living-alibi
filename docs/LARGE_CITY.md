# Large city experimental milestone

441 public 48 m tiles cover 1008 × 1008 m (1.016064 km²). Old tile coordinates, enum IDs and public indexes are preserved. 320 more blocks append to the existing 121. Default population target 160 generated adults, cap 256; new generated indexes above 64 get homes in reachable outer residential blocks and abstract local household purchases. No new address/interior systems. Outer motor roads connect to the existing motor districts; all outer blocks have parking and bicycle racks. Service hubs at outer grid multiples of five support paid refueling from shared finite fuel stock and repair at 06:00–22:00. Client only streams neighboring tiles. Traffic remains local visual simulation; no FPS benchmark, new freight contracts, or public transport vehicles included in this commit.

Municipal shuttle stops at Park, Workshop, Station and outer service hubs. Nearby stops (within seven grid edges) have a 15-minute departure interval, 06:00–22:00, EUR 2 fare, Java-authorized time and arrival. It does not yet render a moving bus or operate in linked-child/adult rebirth mode. Personal cars and bicycles stay parked during the ride. Local resident leisure lookups are precomputed.
