# Autonomous resident entrepreneurship
Residents aged 25–64 with suitable personality, health and savings can buy an
existing insolvent business after two extra listing days. Purchase money goes
to the city and company. Debts survive. Owners retain EUR 180 personal cash,
may hold up to three enterprises, and may invest or take debt-free distributions
weekly. On death the firm becomes unowned. A real working director is appointed
when staffing recovers. The office displays NPC-owned industrial firms.

Experienced programmers may register a consultancy, pay for a lease and
provide startup capital, then recruit a real support assistant. Existing IT
contracts, wages, failure risk and rent renewal apply. A shared client cooldown
prevents different companies selling the same weekly support service repeatedly.
NPC startups are limited to three concurrent companies and one per week.
Acquisitions are also limited to one per week. Both require a working bank clerk.
No new factory buildings, legal inheritance of shares or stock market yet.
