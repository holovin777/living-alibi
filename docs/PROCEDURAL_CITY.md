# Procedural city milestone

The public grid grows from 5×5 to 11×11 (121 tiles, 528×528 m at 48 m per tile). The 96 generated outer tiles have stable identifiers, deterministic Lombard residential blocks, gardens, small plazas, or agricultural scenery. Existing IDs, coordinates, and public-place indexes remain unchanged. Java owns adjacency and charges 8 game minutes per grid edge for travel involving a generated block; previous travel timings remain unchanged. The existing 3×3 streaming window bounds rendered chunks at nine. The map has a local view and a city overview; the travel menu has a text filter. NPCs who use the previous southern leisure routine can occasionally visit an outer block. Population remains at the previous target, with no new interior/address/job simulation or motor routes in the outer blocks. This is an intermediate step, not the 2×2 km target or a measured FPS guarantee.
