# Residential development
Three new residential plots in north and south districts are available to
eligible NPC households. Purchase and all construction stages are paid from
personal savings. Commercial and residential projects share the same daily
crew capacity, alternating priority by day. Construction consumes actual fuel,
steel and building materials. A completed home needs a separate paid
connection by an electrician and plumber, two spare parts, and functioning
existing district power and water/sewer capacity before the household can
relocate. The family moves together using the existing home-unit mechanism,
including stored possessions. Player-controlled residents are excluded.
The office registry displays stages, utility connection and occupancy.
Godot displays new home exteriors in clear mapped spaces. No new road graph,
enterable interiors, dedicated utility mains or additional industrial factories
are introduced in this stage; utility connections use existing district networks.
