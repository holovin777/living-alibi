# Municipal transport development
The north, south and factory districts qualify for a new bus stop at
12 residents plus incoming employees. The city approves one project
at a time and needs two paid construction stages with real builders,
drivers, material and fuel. Existing bus stops and fares stay unchanged.
New stops become valid destinations in the existing bus action system.
NPC bus trips use the timetable duration plus road delay; the vehicle
projection uses the stored trip duration. District road condition
wears daily with demand, and municipal repair crews spend finite cash
and supplies to restore it. The office registry reports construction,
condition, demand, repairs and spending.

No new physical road geometry, traffic signals, standalone bus fleet
schedules or shelters are added. Existing geographic motor routes remain
the source for visual traffic. This is a logical infrastructure upgrade.
