# Social Life v1.2

## Purpose

Validate that independent residents can meet, interact, and build persistent social relationships before expanding the town population or implementing detective interviews.

## Current model

- Fixed participants: Marco Bianchi and Elena Rossi. **New NPCs are not procedurally generated in this version.**
- Physical prerequisite: both must be alive, not traveling, awake and at the same `Place` for at least ten game minutes.
- A seeded deterministic social rule periodically checks their willingness to engage based on sociability, fatigue and tension. Social events are limited to **one per encounter**.
- Interactions are summarized in English; they are not natural-language transcripts. Outcomes include small talk, friendly chat, a tense exchange, or reconciliation.
- Relationships track affinity, trust and tension (0–100), encounter count, conversation count and argument count.
- Relationship state changes can affect Marco's mood, loneliness and stress in simplified game units.
- Each interaction becomes a world event, a separately queryable social event, and a local observation for each present participant. No absent witness is granted that knowledge.
- The RNG state, current meeting start and relationship counters persist across restarts.

## Commands

```text
relationships
relationship marco elena
social-events 20
simulate 14
relationships
social-events 20
```

## Storage

- `town_relationships` stores persistent pair-level social stats and random state.
- `social_interactions` stores a timeline of interactions with time, participants, location and type.
- `town_meta.meeting_since` stores the start of the current co-location.
- All changes are saved in the same SQLite transaction as character state and town logs.

## Deliberate limitations

This is **not** an AI-driven social cognition model. No dialogue generated by OpenAI, no actual information transfer from one citizen's memories to another, no NPC agency to seek out specific friends, no romance and no procedural population generation. The chat summaries represent game-level events rather than actual spoken sentences. `known` still contains developer-mode direct observations, not beliefs or testimony.

## Next step: procedural population

A scalable shared resident model should assign generated citizens persistent identifiers, names, ages, professions, personal needs, traits, schedules, households and relationships. A seeded world generator should produce repeatable towns without manual character authoring. The engine—not a language model—will remain authoritative about facts.
