# Fictional shooting prototype

After obtaining a valid game permit and buying the registered sporting item, select it with Q. Left click (or X) fires in the public scene. The existing near-resident selection is reused; damage is abstract and nonlethal. Public discharge even without a hit generates a police report, revokes the license and confiscates the sporting item. Existing custody and fine systems apply. The workshop kiosk offers a separate lawful practice action from 08:00 to 18:00; it records a persistent practice count and plays the sound, without a public incident. The prototype has no dedicated physical range or target-scoring yet. All rules are fictional. The Java server owns permit checks, possession, damage, reports and practice counters; Godot presents an abstract prop, animation and original sound only after accepted actions.

The sporting item now starts with five abstract game rounds. Practice consumes one; five completed practice actions count as one training session. At the workshop training counter a depleted supply can be replenished for 3 game EUR. A public offense confiscates the item and clears its supply. Existing registered items without a round-count key start at five; zero counts are preserved through copies and SQLite. The first-person HUD shows remaining rounds, and the office shows days until permit expiry. There is no physical loading simulation or target-accuracy score.

Dedicated walkable Armeria and Poligono di tiro facilities are located in the market and workshop districts. Press F at the separate counter. Targets are decorative; practice remains a menu action. Java authorizes by district, not local coordinates. Public shots alarm co-present non-police residents for 60 game minutes; eligible autonomous adults temporarily prefer home. No new ambulance dispatch or physical emergency response yet.

Public incidents now create a one-hour district alert and a public archive notice. Co-present witnesses remember shots through the existing attributed knowledge system. Injury requests are private and persist through the existing world stock table. Available clinic nurses or doctors enable abstract medical dispatch and staged health recovery, capped at the recorded pre-injury health. No physical ambulance vehicle or detailed medical simulation is included. The sports facility has moved clear of the parking area.
