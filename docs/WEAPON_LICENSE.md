# Fictional weapons permit

Apply at the municipal office kiosk during 08:00–17:00 for 10 game EUR. Valid for 30 game days; a clean fictional assault and conviction record is required. The market refuses new training baton and registered sporting weapon purchases without a valid permit. The latter is a persistent abstract registered possession, not a usable firearm. Assault reports revoke the permit and remove the registered sporting weapon; custody retains the previous baton confiscation rules. Existing batons are not retroactively deleted. Expiry prevents further purchases but does not delete possessions. Permits are tied to the visitor incarnation and survive SQLite reloads. Rebirth-linked characters are not supported yet. Costs and rules are fictional game balancing, not Italian law. The office fee and market sale credit the existing retail account as a temporary simplification. No dedicated weapons shop interior yet.
