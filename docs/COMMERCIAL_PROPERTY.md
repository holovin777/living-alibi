# Commercial property and industrial succession
Three finite office plots can be bought by qualified NPC investors with
personal cash. Each plot costs EUR 90. Three distinct daily construction
stages consume one steel and one building-material unit each, and pay a
real working engineer and both suppliers. No progress without funds,
materials or available crew. At most one stage per town/day.

Completed offices can receive real IT company lease payments instead of
the municipal shared-office account. A lease covers seven days, with one
tenant per property. Unavailable offices fall back to the municipal lease.
Existing factory ownership and commercial plots pass to the oldest living
child of a deceased NPC owner, if present, otherwise become unowned.
Debt remains with the company. Minor heirs cannot autonomously develop
the property. This is simplified single-heir succession, not legal advice.

The office action panel displays commercial plots, owners, stage and leases.
Sites are currently abstract registry entries, not new 3D geometry or
walkable buildings. Player plot transactions and construction of new
industrial production plants are not implemented here. Existing visitor
construction remains independent.
