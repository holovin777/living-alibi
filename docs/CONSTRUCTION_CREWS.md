# Commercial construction crews and scenery

Architects, builders and construction truck drivers are new staffed roles.
The first stage requires an architect; all stages require a builder and
driver. A contractor fee, individual wages, actual fuel and the existing
industrial material suppliers are paid from the owner's personal cash.
No contract runs if supplies, crew or money are missing. Roles are paid
per completed stage, not by the generic placeholder hourly wage.

The Godot client projects purchased commercial sites near existing district
anchors only when clear space exists in the imported map. The three
stages show a foundation, walls and finished office facade; unfinished
sites have a static crane, barriers and pallets. They are visual projections
of persisted state, not new independently enterable workplaces. Truck
drivers and fuel are modeled economically, not as a routed delivery vehicle.
