# Autonomous enterprise management

Staffed solvency accounts appoint working NPCs to director, department lead,
accounting and HR responsibilities. Public firms choose an eligible working
director; an acquired company requires the player's explicit director appointment.
Roles are additional responsibilities, not new occupations or extra salaries.
Small firms share responsibilities. No ghost staff are created.

Directors review available cash and wage liabilities and authorize recruitment
only if a vacancy and an eight-hour payroll reserve exist. A department head
can mentor a real novice. Accountants review the actual corporate balance and
wage arrears. HR selects a qualified unemployed resident, records a next-day
appointment, and rechecks eligibility, funds, vacancies and interview aptitude
before signing an existing employment contract. Other job opportunities can
intervene. Delegated owned firms use this route instead of instant routine hiring.
Public firms retain baseline staffing for compatibility, with supplementary HR.
Managers must be on duty and not in a player conversation. Daily markers prevent
repeat decisions during reloads. Owner removal clears delegated decisions.

A rolling seven-day cash report is generated at 06:30, before morning settlements,
using real balances and recorded cash wages/arrears payments. Net cash movement
includes all transfers (including borrowing and investment), and is NOT profit.
No report is fabricated for a partial initial day. The report is not full double-entry
accounting. All new state uses the existing persisted industryStocks map.

At an office computer, E opens the workstation with existing owner actions,
delegated roles, decisions, interviews, audits and the cash reports for owned
solvency firms. English and Italian labels are provided. No access to other
companies' private reports in this UI. Independent workshop, IT and stall
business accounts are not yet covered by this subsystem.
