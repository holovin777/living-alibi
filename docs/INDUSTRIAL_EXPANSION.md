# Industrial expansion
Two finite independent expansion sites (cement and building materials)
become investment opportunities after three consecutive days of low
stock while construction projects remain active. NPC owners pay EUR 120
for land and transfer EUR 80 of their money into a separate operating
account. Three construction stages require the existing shared crew,
fuel and materials. A new role becomes funded only after completion.
New plants have separate inventory and batch wages. Staffed imports,
fuel, power, optional gas and cement input are paid before production.
The incumbent distributor can buy stock at a premium only during
a shortage, retaining its working reserve. No stock or money is
generated by founding a company. The premium may make redistribution
unprofitable; it is a bounded emergency supply model, not price bidding.
Completed plants have industrial exterior scenery and registry data.
No new road network, player entry, full governance integration or
unlimited procedural sites yet.
