# Municipal infrastructure projects
The city monitors daily service pressure, then approves one project at a
time after four consecutive pressure days (two under an infrastructure
election programme). Approval requires a budget sufficient for all three
stages above the municipal reserve, but does not escrow it. Work requires
available utility technician, builder and driver, steel, construction
material, fuel and EUR 18 per stage. Spending is transferred to real
workers and suppliers. No work is possible below the municipal reserve.
Public and private construction share crew capacity.

Completed solar extension increases storage capacity by 12 and seasonal
solar generation by 50%. North/south substation upgrades restore feeder
condition and halve daily wear. Water extension adds 24 storage units
and 8 daily treatment units; sewer extension adds 16 storage and 6 daily
processing units. All use the existing utility operating checks and paid
staff. New connected homes add to daily municipal water and power demand.

Godot displays the projects as exterior scenery near clear map locations.
No new routable roads, dedicated bus routes, physical electrical solver or
enterable utility buildings are introduced in this phase.
