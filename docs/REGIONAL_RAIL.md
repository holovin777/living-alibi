# Regional rail
The municipality can purchase a regional rail service (EUR 80 rolling
stock and EUR 40 operating cash) with its real budget. Two scheduled
return services connect Vigevano to off-map Pavia and Milano. Drivers
and freight operators are real staffed roles; station attendants are
also required. Each departure consumes paid electricity when enabled.
Up to 16 eligible residents already present at the station may buy
EUR 8 return tickets. They are absent from ordinary local routines
until the return time. No imaginary passengers are generated.

On a paid freight order, the existing regional materials supplier
receives payment for a bounded import batch. The manifest is retained
until arrival and only then placed in existing industrial stocks,
waiting if storage is full. The service receives a handling fee.
No separate 3D train, player intercity travel or new city maps are
implemented. Timetables and capacities are fictional game values.
