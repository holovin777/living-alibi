# Living Alibi — Roadmap

The eventual game is an emergent detective simulation. The player investigates events in a town where residents continue to pursue their own lives independently.

## Completed

### Marco v1.0 — Autonomous console resident

- Marco's needs, economy, aging and mortality
- SQLite persistence and optional OpenAI dialogue
- Time skipping, controlled live mode and optional catch-up on reopening

### The Living Town v1.1 — First witness infrastructure

- Second named resident (Elena Rossi), with a simplified independent state
- Two homes and four public places, timed travel
- Separate authoritative town events and personal observation logs
- SQLite migration without deleting Marco's history
- Tests for independent life state, meetings, and observation isolation

### Social Life v1.2 — First autonomous relationships

- Rule-based NPC-to-NPC conversations requiring actual co-location
- Affinity, trust, tension, small talk, arguments and reconciliation
- Persistent relationship progress and private observations of social encounters
- Deterministic social rules and smoke tests, without API cost

### Population Generation v1.3 — First procedurally generated citizens

- Reproducible names and stable citizen IDs, traits, professions, abstract home/work locations and simple life goals
- Rule-driven activity, travel, needs, work, shopping, mortality and SQLite persistence for generated citizens
- A conservative cap of 40 generated residents and CLI tools to inspect population and resident state
- Note: no generated-citizen friendship network, births or spontaneous immigration yet

### Private Lives v1.4 — Individual apartments and scene-based witnessing

- Every citizen has a stable private home unit, distinct from the wider residential place.
- Witnesses are assigned only when their current public/private space matches the authoritative event scene.
- SQLite stores new event `space_id` and upgrades v1.3 world saves additively.
- Individualized need phases reduce synchronized resident behavior.
- No room geometry, audibility or household cohabitation yet.

### Social Network v1.5 — Autonomous relationships between residents

- New pairs form only after meeting in the **same exact scene** (not merely the same building).
- Their persistent trust, affinity and tension change through rule-driven conversations.
- A single pair has at most one approach roll per day; individual conversations are not generated by paid AI.
- The original Marco/Elena relationship survives the upgrade without rewriting prior history.
- No exchange of secondhand knowledge, uncertain beliefs, manipulation or independent testimony yet.

### Memory & Beliefs v1.6 — Attributed testimony and rumor circulation

- Distinguish witnessed noteworthy events (`DIRECT`) from claims received in conversation (`HEARD`).
- Record source, original event, relay depth and a simple confidence heuristic for each claim.
- Relay at most one unshared claim per actual conversation and preserve private scene boundaries.
- Persist claims and the history of information exchanges to new SQLite tables.
- **Not implemented:** deliberate deception, mistaken identity, distortion, evidence search or interviews by the detective.

### Evidence & Alibis v1.9 — Detective evidence and witness checking

- Preserve contemporary scene and location evidence, ambiguous physical clues, and interview-dependent corroborated alibis.
- Legacy events remain historically uncertain rather than having invented snapshots.

### Motives & Opportunity v1.10 — Contextual incidents

- Replace fixed-weekday thefts with probabilistic, time-local incentives and opportunities.
- Separate secret engine motives from broad detective-accessible background clues.
- Preserve historical cases through nullable columns and new snapshot tables.

### Economy & Debts v1.11 — Household arrears and trusted lending

- Recurring housing and repair charges for generated citizens, with explicit unpaid balances.
- Small zero-interest personal lending, repayments, due dates and consequences for overdue contracts.
- Transaction history and contract state persist; financial strain affects only future incident snapshots.
- Developer diagnostics are kept separate from detective-visible background clues.

### Employment & Life Events v1.12 — Income interruptions and brief illnesses

- Generated citizens can lose work, seek new employment, change occupations, and earn wages only while employed and present at a workplace.
- Short illness episodes suspend work and charge a medical expense through the existing household arrears system.
- Career and health events have append-only SQLite history; no personal history is inferred retroactively for pre-v1.12 saves.
- Existing investigator case snapshots remain unchanged, and these personal diagnostics are not detective evidence.

### Physical Property v1.13 — Real belongings and custody

- Stable wallet, phone and bicycle IDs with legal owner, actual holder and location (current carrier vs private home storage).
- Wallet-theft cases now involve a specific carried object; victim retains legal ownership. Purchase a distinct replacement after a theft.
- Append-only custody audit and private case-to-object linkage; historical cases are preserved without fabricated property records.
- Developer-only inventory views never substitute for detective evidence. Item seizure/release is implemented in v1.14; gifts/trades and detailed item forensics are future features.

### Recovery & Chain of Custody v1.14 — Evidence-guided searches

- An investigator can search a resident after interviewing enough people and establishing a named or non-unique clothing lead.
- The item is secured in an evidence room with append-only custody history, and returned only after resolution.
- Search outcomes survive SQLite restarts and repeated actions are idempotent.
- A two-week repeat-victim cooldown reduces unrealistic wallet-theft loops without modifying old reports.

### Anonymous Visitor v1.15 — Optional investigation and voluntary identity

- A player can move freely between public places and stay anonymous as long as they wish.
- Resident contacts preserve only names explicitly volunteered to that resident, never a global player ID/name.
- No missions are required, and the simulation continues while the visitor explores.
- Future: NPCs independently notice and approach visitors, fuller player life simulation,
  deeper contact memory, real anonymity-respecting dialogues, and a Godot visual client.

## In progress

### Player Daily Life v1.16 — Physical condition and short rest

- Persistent hunger, thirst, fatigue and health for the anonymous player.
- Clock-driven needs continue during walks, waits, live mode and offline replay.
- `needs` and `rest` console commands; public breaks consume world time.
- `sleep [hours]` works in any accessible public area, with an early
  place-based exposure/health heuristic, persistent visitor mortality and
  no mandatory property ownership. Temperature, rain and snow now affect
  outdoor risk. Next: shelters, medicine and player access to the town economy.
- Death automatically starts a new anonymous visitor in the same continuous
  world; previous player lives and disclosed per-resident identities remain
  archived without leaking into the new person's memories.
- Details: [Player Daily Life](PLAYER_DAILY_LIFE.md).
- Early natural-resource slice: finite river/puddle water and wild berries, local
  harvesting and saved depletion ([Natural Resources](NATURAL_RESOURCES.md)).
  Industrial NPC extraction and basic factories now exist
  ([Industry](INDUSTRY.md)); wildlife, shelter, and advanced enterprises
  with real energy, contracts and supply pricing remain future work.

### Player freedom: jobs, education and careers (v1.16 draft)

- New anonymous visitor starts **in the park with 50 EUR** and no occupation,
  criminal case, detective status or preassigned mission.
- `jobs`, `apply <job>`, `work`, `study engineering`,
  `study investigation`, and `career` form the first voluntary career loop.
- Shop assistant, workshop technician, junior engineer and private investigator
  have workplace, qualification and bounded salary requirements. Education
  costs tuition and takes actual world time; wages draw from finite institutional
  accounts persisted in SQLite. This is an initial employer-budget model,
  not yet the full NPC business economy.
- Workshop technicians and engineers must now complete a **physical work
  order** (inspection, material-consuming repair or upgrade) before their
  paid technical shift. The park water pump and public street lighting have
  persistent condition, shared finite component stocks, daily wear and
  archived completion state. Repeated work will eventually need new
  material supply chains. A first staffed quarry → factory → repair
  warehouse supply chain now delivers finite metal and spare parts, but
  import/recycling after deposit exhaustion is future work. See
  [Technical Work](TECHNICAL_WORK.md) and [Industry](INDUSTRY.md).
- Shared city water and lighting now alter both NPC well-being and **NPC choices**:
  the park's finite public fountain gives thirsty residents a cheaper
  destination than the market, while poor street lights send some evening
  visitors to the cafe rather than the park. Actual source use and citizen
  travel remain world events; see [City Utilities](CITY_UTILITIES.md).
- A successor visitor never inherits an old career, money or qualifications.
- Spring API adds job listings and matching voluntary player actions.
- See [Player Careers](PLAYER_CAREERS.md).

### Finite industry and material supply (v1.16 draft)

- A first-run town has miners and factory machinists among 12 procedural
  residents. They commute, work, earn NPC wages and can stop producing due
  to illness, unemployment, death, weekends or absent resources.
- Quarry has finite underground iron and copper deposits. NPCs mine real
  quantities, transport ore to a factory, refine steel and copper, produce
  spare parts, and deliver bounded batches to the existing city maintenance
  stock. Each stage requires a working NPC at its real workplace.
- All industrial inventories and daily processing markers persist in SQLite;
  no repeated loading creates additional minerals or duplicate shipments.
  Spring exposes `GET /api/v1/industry`, the console has `industry`,
  and Godot offers a public industrial dashboard and Quarry/Factory districts.
- Quarry miners, transport drivers and factory machinists now receive
  salaries from finite company accounts; the factory pays for ore and
  city maintenance pays for deliveries. Bankrupt businesses can lay
  off employees, who then search for other local work.
- The player can apply for quarry/factory jobs and produce real materials
  during paid two-hour shifts. A two-hour geological survey may unlock
  one hidden finite western deposit.
- A limited three-unit freight truck takes 30 game minutes between
  dispatch and arrival. Factory workers can recycle two finite scrap
  units into one steel unit, with lossy recovery.
- Fully modeled wages in other economic sectors, animated trucks,
  enterprise ownership, external trade and energy consumption are
  future work. See [Industry](INDUSTRY.md).
  See [Industry](INDUSTRY.md).

### New world calendar and Godot API bridge (v1.16 draft)

- Four recurring seasons follow the authoritative 365-day Java clock,
  beginning in spring. Deterministic daily rain/snow/cloud/clear skies and
  temperature affect outdoor sleep, puddles and seasonal berries. Detailed
  weather fronts and NPC weather-aware decisions remain future work.
  See [Weather](WEATHER.md).
- The first launch with no saved database automatically creates a playable
  world. The Spring server starts with Marco, Elena and twelve generated
  residents, rather than requiring manual developer commands.
- Standalone Spring Boot 4.2 API module under `server/` shares the same Java
  simulation and SQLite storage. `GET /api/v1/world` exposes time, season,
  weather and temperature, nearby NPCs, resources and visitor condition. Commands
  over HTTP validate movement, rest, sleep, drinking, gathering and waiting.
- Godot 4.6.1 now renders **several 3D public districts** with a
  pedestrian avatar, travel gate (E) and local career/resource kiosk (F).
  The Java API supplies canonical place IDs and travel time, commits movement
  before Godot redraws the area, and validates applications, study and shifts.
  Godot headless CI exercises park → market → school → workshop scene
  transitions and context-specific kiosk options. Per-meter navigation,
  real connected streets, NPC meshes and multiplayer are future work.
- Details: [Godot / Spring Boot integration](GODOT_INTEGRATION.md).

## Proposed, not implemented

1. **Shared agent engine:** move Marco, Elena and procedural residents to the same extensible NPC engine; time/position-aware action execution.
2. **Dynamic society:** births and migration, shared households, detailed room geometry, and scalable work schedules. General relationships are implemented at a basic level.
3. **Advanced memory and belief:** basic attributed information exchange is implemented; deception and simple motive pressure are implemented; fading memory, genuine contradictions, and deep psychological drivers remain to be developed.
4. **Incidents and evidence:** theft/lost property, alibis, trails, witnesses, event causality, evidence acquisition.
5. **Playable detective:** first console interviews, case summaries, background leads, and accusations are implemented; free walking has been added for the visitor, while rich deduction tools, case linkage, and fair legal outcomes remain future work.
6. **Organized crime and social power:** emergent groups with ranks, territorial influence, debts, intimidation and leverage over witnesses. Authority must be built through interactions and history, never attached as a static "criminal" flag.
7. **Human vulnerability and addiction:** nuanced dependency, harm, support and recovery mechanics grounded in character histories; substance use or a health vulnerability must never automatically imply violence or criminal behavior.
8. **Continuous hosting and presentation:** headless daemon first, then an optional Godot client that reads the same authoritative events without controlling world truth.

## Principles

- The simulation, not the language model, determines world truth and physical outcomes.
- Every resident knows only what they could have witnessed or learned.
- No mandatory paid AI call during time steps.
- Persistent history outlives individual residents.
- API secrets and local conversation history never belong in the public Git repository.

### First Investigation v1.8 — Console prototype

- A living, co-present population can create an authoritative missing-wallet incident.
- The detective sees a case report, not the culprit; can inspect the scene, record attributed interviews and accuse a suspect.
- Evidence, case history and outcomes persist separately from the simulation's hidden truth.
- Further work: richer crime motives, scene reconstruction, time-limited travel/interviews, forensics, evidence-based verdicts, multi-case causality.
